Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 16, 2025

DOODL. is built for sharing doodles with friends and family in small groups. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use DOODL.

1. What we collect

We collect only what we need to run DOODL:

  • Account details: username, a pairing/recovery code, profile ID, and optional avatar URL.
  • Group info: the groups you create or join and group membership.
  • Doodles: doodles you create and send (including image/base64 content).
  • Activity data: timestamps for doodles and basic “last active” signals (to show online/offline status).
  • Push tokens: APNs device tokens and environment (sandbox/production) to deliver notifications.
  • Basic app info: device/app version and limited logs for reliability and support.

2. How we use data

  • Operate the app: create accounts, join groups, sync doodles, and show inbox/unread counts.
  • Notifications: send push notifications when new doodles arrive.
  • Reliability & security: prevent abuse and troubleshoot issues.
  • Product improvement: improve performance and fix bugs (using limited, privacy-focused signals).

3. How we store data

DOODL. uses managed cloud services (including Supabase) to store and sync your data. Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted to the minimum needed to operate the service.

If you upload an avatar, it may be stored in managed object storage. Doodles and metadata are stored in the database.

4. Sharing

DOODL. does not sell your personal data. We share limited information only with service providers that help run the app, such as:

  • Supabase (database and storage)
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) (notification delivery)

We may also share information if required by law or to protect users and the service.

5. Your choices & control

  • Edit your profile: you can change your username and avatar in the app (if available).
  • Notifications: you can enable/disable notifications in iOS settings.
  • Delete your account: you can request deletion from within the app (if available) or by contacting us.

6. Retention

We keep your data for as long as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we remove your profile and associated data from our active systems, subject to technical limitations and legal requirements.

We may retain minimal records (for example, logs or security records) if required to comply with law or prevent abuse.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as encryption in transit, access controls, and monitoring to protect your data. No system is perfect, so please keep your device secure and keep DOODL. updated.

8. Children

DOODL. is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will remove it.

9. International use

Your data may be processed and stored in countries other than where you live. We apply the protections described in this policy regardless of location.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If changes are significant, we will provide notice in the app. Continued use of DOODL. after changes means you accept the updated policy.
